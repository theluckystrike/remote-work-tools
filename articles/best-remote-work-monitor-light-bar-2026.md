## Overview

Monitor light bars reduce eye strain during long work sessions by eliminating the contrast between bright monitor and dark desk surroundings. Remote workers spend 8+ hours daily staring at screens. Proper ambient lighting decreases headaches, eye fatigue, and improves focus. This guide compares actual monitor light bars with pricing, brightness, and color temperature specs.

## The Eye Strain Problem

Direct monitor light in darkness creates:
- Pupil dilation/contraction stress (accommodation lag)
- Higher melanin production (disrupts circadian rhythm)
- Reduced blink rate (dry eyes)
- Peripheral visual discomfort

Monitor light bars solve this by:
- Reducing contrast between monitor and desk
- Providing consistent ambient lighting
- Matching monitor color temperature
- Eliminating harsh shadows

Studies show 40% reduction in eye strain with proper monitor lighting.

## Installation Guide: BenQ ScreenBar Pro (Most Popular)

**What's Included:**
- Light bar unit
- USB-C cable (1.5m)
- Adhesive mounting bracket
- Cloth for cleaning

**Setup Steps:**

1. **Clean monitor top edge** with provided cloth (removes dust, ensures adhesive grip)

2. **Attach mounting bracket** to monitor top center (self-adhesive, press for 10 seconds)

3. **Mount light bar onto bracket** (slots into groove, secure with friction)

4. **Connect USB-C cable:**
   - To monitor USB hub (if available)
   - To powered USB hub on desk
   - To laptop USB-C port (some power loss on laptops)

5. **Download app:**
   - BenQ ScreenBar app (iOS/Android/Windows/Mac)
   - Log in or use local mode
   - Grant Bluetooth permission

6. **Calibrate:** App offers preset brightness/warmth; adjust to desk lighting

**Cable Management:** Use adhesive clips to route USB cable along monitor arm (minimize desktop clutter).

**Positioning:** Mount on top edge of monitor bezel, centered. Ensures light reflects downward and sideways evenly.

## Troubleshooting

**Light bar dims unexpectedly:**
- Check auto-dimming setting (turn off if in bright room)
- Verify ambient light sensor not blocked

**Colors look washed out:**
- Increase brightness (80-90%)
- Verify color temperature (try 5000K)
- Check monitor brightness (should be 50% at least)

**USB power insufficient:**
- Use powered USB hub instead of laptop port
- Monitor USB hub may not provide sufficient current (5V/1A needed)

**App won't connect:**
- Restart app + light bar
- Forget Bluetooth device, re-pair
- Factory reset light bar (check manual for procedure)

**Flickering visible:**
- Check for PWM-based competitors (BenQ uses DC, no flicker)
- Verify USB cable is fully inserted
- Try different USB port
